Privacy Policy

Detailed, full legal version - For Meta App Review & End Users
Updated: August 2026
1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to the applications and services of EZI Tech Solutions ("we", "our"). Protecting your personal data and privacy is our highest commitment.

This Privacy Policy is drafted to explain in the most transparent, detailed and comprehensive way how we collect, use, store, process and protect your information when you access the website, use the software, or connect your Facebook/Meta account with our system (collectively referred to as "Services").

This Policy strictly complies with current privacy regulations, including the Meta App Developer Policies, as well as general data protection standards. Your continued use of the Services and allowing login via Facebook means that you have read, understood and fully agreed to the terms stated below.

2. Information and Data we collect

To provide you with a complete Service (including Fanpage management, ads synchronization, automated message replies), we only collect the minimum necessary data. This data is divided into the following groups:

2.1. Data from Facebook Account (Via Meta Graph API)

When you perform the "Login with Facebook" action and grant permissions to the application, we may access the following data based on your permission:

  • Basic Identity Data: Display name, profile picture, email address, and Facebook User ID.
  • Fanpage Management Data (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts): List of Pages you manage, Page ID, Page name, Page category, post content, images/videos you posted on the Page, likes, comments, and the Page's Access Token to maintain the connection.
  • Inbox Data (pages_messaging): History of conversations, text messages, image attachments that customers send to your Page, as well as messages you send through our system.
  • Ads and Business Data (ads_management, business_management, ads_read): Ad Account ID, Business Manager ID, campaign configurations, ad sets, ad creatives, and performance measurement metrics (Reach, Impressions, Cost, Clicks, Conversions).

2.2. Data Provided Directly by You

Includes the information you fill in the account registration form on our system, contact phone number, company name, tax code (if any for invoicing), and support request contents via email/chat.

2.3. Automatically Collected Data (Log & Usage Data)

When you use the application, our system automatically records technical information such as: IP address, browser type, operating system, access time, pages/functions you clicked, and error logs to serve for system troubleshooting.

3. Purpose of data use

All data collected from you and from Meta API is used by us exclusively for lawful purposes to provide and improve Service quality. Specifically:

  • Providing core features: Allows you to view reports, post articles, read and automate replies to comments/messages on Fanpage directly from our platform.
  • Ads Management and Optimization: Aggregates metrics from Meta Ads to draw campaign performance report charts (Campaign Insights), supporting you in planning scripts and creating new ad campaigns directly on our interface quickly.
  • Maintenance and Authentication: Uses Access Tokens to maintain a secure login session between our system and Meta's system, ensuring your workflow is uninterrupted.
  • Service Improvement: Analyzes anonymous usage data (not associated with identity) to detect software bugs and upgrade the user interface (UX/UI).
  • Customer Support: Uses your email or phone number to send account status notifications, security alerts, or respond to technical support requests from you.
4. Sharing and Disclosing data to Third parties

We absolutely respect privacy and consider your data as an asset that needs to be strictly protected.

  • No Data Selling Commitment: We DO NOT SELL, rent, exchange, or monetize any of your personal data or Facebook data to any third party, data broker, or advertising company.
  • Cloud Providers: Your data is stored on enterprise-grade secure cloud servers (such as AWS, Google Cloud). These partners are bound by strict security contracts and do not have access to your data to use for their own purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: We reserve the right to disclose your data when forced to do so by a lawful request from a Court, investigating agency, or to comply with legal regulations to prevent fraud, protect the legitimate rights of EZI Tech Solutions and the safety of other users.
5. Data Storage and Security

We apply software industry security standards to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

  • Transmission Encryption: All data communication streams between your browser, our servers and Meta are encrypted according to the Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) standard.
  • Storage Encryption: Highly critical sensitive data such as Facebook Access Tokens, Account Passwords, payment information are one-way encrypted (hashing) or asymmetrically encrypted at the Database layer.
  • Retention Policy:
    • We only retain your data for as long as your account is active or necessary to perform contractual obligations.
    • Content history (Posts/Messages/Ads Data) is temporarily stored to serve report display. If you stop using the service or disconnect the Fanpage/Ad Account, the system will automatically stop collecting new data.
    • System logs will typically be deleted or anonymized after 90 days.
6. User Rights

No matter what region you are in, we grant you the highest level of control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to log into the application and view all the data we are storing about you and the Fanpages you have connected.
  • Right to Edit: You can update, modify your personal profile information at any time through the Account Settings section.
  • Right to Opt-out: You can unsubscribe from receiving marketing emails or non-essential notifications from us.
  • Right to Erasure (Delete): You have the right to request complete and permanent deletion of your data from our system. (Please see details in Section 7).
7. Data Deletion Instructions

If you no longer wish to use the application and want to delete all data we have collected from your Facebook, you can follow this transparent process:

Method 1: Immediate Deletion via Facebook (Recommended)

  1. Log into your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. Select Apps and Websites or Business Integrations.
  4. Find our app in the list, select Remove.
    As soon as you do this, the Access Tokens we hold will immediately be invalidated, and our system will no longer be able to access any further data from your Facebook.

Method 2: Send a Data Deletion Request to our servers

To ensure all historical data (including logs, backups) is wiped clean from our Database:

  1. Access the Data Deletion Instructions page on our website.
  2. Fill in your email or Account ID in the data deletion request form.
  3. We will receive, verify the request and proceed to permanently delete all related data within a maximum of 7 working days. You will receive a confirmation email when the deletion process is complete.
8. Use of Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use Cookies (small text files saved on your device) and similar technologies (like LocalStorage, SessionStorage) solely for the purpose of maintaining login sessions (Authentication), remembering language/interface preferences, and ensuring network security against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks. You can perfectly configure your browser to reject all cookies, however, this may cause some security features of the Service to not function correctly.

9. Policy Updates and Changes

We reserve the right to modify and update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in technology, law, or Service features. Any significant changes will be clearly notified to you via email (if you have provided it) or by a prominent notice directly on the application interface before the change takes effect. The "Last Updated" date at the top of the page will let you know when this policy was last amended.

10. Contact Information

We are always listening and ready to answer any questions you may have about this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions, complaints, or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer via:

  • Support & Legal Email: contact@ezisolutions.tech
  • Headquarters Address: EZI Tech Solutions Headquarter, Viet Nam.
