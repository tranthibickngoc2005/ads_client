Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 06, 2026

This service helps authorized business users connect Facebook Pages, publish Page posts, manage Messenger conversations, connect Meta Business / Ad Accounts, and optimize advertising campaigns. We only request Facebook data that is required to provide these features.

Data We Process

  • Facebook Page identifiers, Page names, categories, granted permissions, and connection status.
  • Page posts created through this service, including text content, public image URLs, publish status, and errors.
  • Page conversations and messages needed for two-way live customer support workflows.
  • Meta Business Account identifiers, Ad Account IDs, campaign configurations, and performance insights (reach, impressions, spend, clicks, conversions).
  • Targeting location and demographic audience parameters used for reach estimations via Meta Marketing API.
  • Basic demo account credentials used to let Meta reviewers access the review environment.

How We Use Data

Data is used to show connected Pages, publish content requested by the user, display inbox conversations, send replies, synchronize ad accounts and campaign analytics, maintain audit history, and support data deletion requests. We do not sell Facebook data to third parties.

Retention and Deletion

Page connection records, posts, conversations, messages, and ad campaign configurations are retained only while needed for business workflows or legal/audit requirements. Users can disconnect a Page or Ad Account in the app or request data deletion through the public deletion page.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the app owner at contact@ezisolutions.tech or submit a deletion request from the data deletion page.
